# EduSphere University Examination Conduct & Assessment Rules 2026

**Document ID**: `univ-reg-exam-2026`  
**Category**: `examination_rules`  
**Applicability**: All University Students & Faculty Evaluators  
**Academic Year**: 2025–2026  
**Source Authority**: Office of the Controller of Examinations (CoE)  

---

## Section 1: Assessment Structure & Weightage
1.1. Course evaluation consists of two statutory components:
- **Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA)**: **40% weightage** (40 Marks).
  - Internal Assessment Tests (IAT-1 & IAT-2): 20 Marks.
  - Quizzes and Objective Online Assessments: 10 Marks.
  - Assignments, Mini-Projects, and Case Studies: 10 Marks.
- **End-Semester Examination (ESE)**: **60% weightage** (60 Marks).
1.2. Minimum Passing Thresholds:
- A candidate must obtain a minimum of **45% marks in the End-Semester Examination** (27 out of 60).
- An aggregate score (CIA + ESE) of **minimum 50%** is mandatory to earn credits in any theory or practical course.

---

## Section 2: Examination Eligibility & Hall Ticket Issuance
2.1. Hall tickets are issued digitally through the EduSphere Student Portal five (5) days before the commencement of examinations.
2.2. A student is disqualified from hall ticket download if:
- Aggregate subject attendance is less than 75% without authorized condonation.
- Outstanding tuition or laboratory dues remain unresolved with the accounts section.
- Active disciplinary suspension is recorded in the student profile.

---

## Section 3: Malpractice Policy & Disciplinary Penalties
3.1. Possession of unauthorized materials (handwritten notes, smartwatches, smartphones, programmed calculators, or digital storage media) inside the examination hall constitutes Level-1 Academic Malpractice.
3.2. **Penalties**:
- **Level 1 (Possession of unauthorized material)**: Cancellation of the examination in that particular subject and award of zero marks.
- **Level 2 (Copying / Exchanging papers)**: Cancellation of all registered examinations in the current semester and debarment from the subsequent semester examination.
- **Level 3 (Impersonation / System tampering)**: Immediate rustication from the university for one full academic year and reporting to the State Disciplinary Committee.

---

## Section 4: Revaluation & Answer Script Transparency
4.1. Students may apply for digital photocopy verification of evaluated answer scripts within seven (7) days of result publication upon payment of ₹500 per paper.
4.2. Formal revaluation may be submitted within five (5) days following photocopy receipt. If the revaluation mark exceeds the original mark by 15% or more, the entire revaluation fee is refunded to the student.
