# EduSphere University Attendance Regulations 2026

**Document ID**: `univ-reg-att-2026`  
**Category**: `institutional_policy`  
**Applicability**: All Undergraduate and Postgraduate Engineering Departments  
**Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
**Source Authority**: Academic Senate & Office of the Controller of Examinations  

---

## Section 1: Mandatory Attendance Requirement
1.1. Every enrolled student must maintain a minimum attendance of **75%** in each registered course (theory, laboratory, and integrated practicals) during the semester to become eligible to appear for the End-Semester Examinations (ESE).  
1.2. Attendance calculation is determined as:
$$\text{Attendance Percentage} = \left( \frac{\text{Total Lectures / Lab Sessions Attended}}{\text{Total Conducted Periods}} \right) \times 100$$
1.3. Students possessing attendance between **75.0% and 100%** are classified as in the **Safe Zone** and are automatically cleared for examination hall ticket issuance.

---

## Section 2: Medical Condonation & Exemption Policy
2.1. A student whose attendance falls between **65.0% and 74.9%** may apply for **Condonation of Attendance Shortage** strictly on valid grounds:
- Serious hospitalization or medical illness supported by a registered medical practitioner's certificate.
- Authorized institutional representation in National/State sports tournaments, NCC/NSS camps, or official university hackathons.
2.2. **Submission Deadline**: The medical leave application and medical fitness certificate must be submitted to the Head of Department (HoD) within **three (3) working days** of returning to campus. Applications submitted after three days shall be summarily rejected.
2.3. **Condonation Fee**: A prescribed non-refundable condonation processing fee of ₹1,500 per subject must be remitted following HoD recommendation and Dean (Academics) approval.
2.4. Under no circumstances can attendance shortage below 65.0% be condoned on medical grounds.

---

## Section 3: Attendance Shortage & Detention Regulations
3.1. Any student whose aggregate subject attendance is **below 65.0%** shall be declared **Detained due to Attendance Shortage (DAS)** in that specific course.
3.2. A detained student is prohibited from appearing in the End-Semester Examination for that course and will be awarded a grade of `'SA'` (Shortage of Attendance).
3.3. **Remediation**: The student must re-register for the detained subject in the subsequent supplementary summer semester or when the course is next offered by the department.

---

## Section 4: On-Duty (OD) Attendance Tracking
4.1. Students participating in approved co-curricular competitions, technical symposiums, and placement drives are eligible for On-Duty (OD) credit up to a maximum of **10 working days per semester**.
4.2. OD requests must be pre-approved by the Faculty Advisor and confirmed through the EduSphere Faculty Portal prior to the event.
