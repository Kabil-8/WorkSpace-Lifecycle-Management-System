## Section 2: Algorithmic Prerequisites & Learning Pathways
2.1. **Course CS501 (Algorithms)**:
- **Module 1**: Asymptotic notations, recurrence relations, Master Theorem.
- **Module 2**: Divide and Conquer (MergeSort, QuickSort, Closest Pair).
- **Module 3**: **Dynamic Programming**:
  - *Strict Prerequisite Rule*: Students must master **Recursion** and **Divide & Conquer** before advancing to multi-dimensional Dynamic Programming (Knapsack, Matrix Chain Multiplication, Bellman-Ford).
  - Students exhibiting difficulty in DP memoization must review recursive tree evaluation and state space formulation.
- **Module 4**: Greedy Algorithms & Minimum Spanning Trees (Kruskal, Prim).
- **Module 5**: NP-Completeness, Reductions, and Approximation Algorithms.

## Section 3: Professional Electives Track (Semester 6 & 7)
3.1. **Track A (Artificial Intelligence & Data)**: Machine Learning (CS611), Deep Learning & NLP (CS711), Big Data Analytics (CS712).  
3.2. **Track B (Systems & Cloud)**: Distributed Systems (CS621), Cloud Computing & DevOps (CS721), High Performance Computing (CS722).  
3.3. **Track C (Cybersecurity)**: Cryptography & Network Security (CS631), Ethical Hacking & Digital Forensics (CS731).  

3.4. **Elective Selection Rule**: Students must choose at least three courses from one designated specialization track to qualify for department honors in that domain.
