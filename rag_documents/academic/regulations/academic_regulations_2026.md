# EduSphere Academic Regulations & Grading System 2026

**Document ID**: `univ-reg-acad-2026`  
**Category**: `academic_regulations`  
**Applicability**: B.Tech / B.E. Degree Programmes  
**Grading Model**: 10-Point Relative Grading System (UGC / AICTE Compliant)  
**Effective Regulation**: Regulation R2026  

---

## Section 1: Letter Grades & Grade Points
1.1. Student performance in each course is quantified using a 10-point absolute/relative letter grading scale:

| Letter Grade | Performance Level | Grade Point | Raw Mark Range (Standard) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **O** | Outstanding | 10 | 90 – 100 |
| **A+** | Excellent | 9 | 80 – 89 |
| **A** | Very Good | 8 | 70 – 79 |
| **B+** | Good | 7 | 60 – 69 |
| **B** | Above Average | 6 | 55 – 59 |
| **C** | Pass | 5 | 50 – 54 |
| **RA / F** | Re-Appear (Failed) | 0 | < 50 |
| **SA** | Shortage of Attendance | 0 | Detained (< 65%) |
| **W** | Withdrawal | 0 | Officially Withdrawn |

1.2. The minimum passing grade in any individual course is `'C'` (Grade Point 5). Any grade below 5 is treated as an active backlog.

---

## Section 2: SGPA and CGPA Computation
2.1. **Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA)**:
$$\text{SGPA} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} (C_i \times G_i)}{\sum_{i=1}^{n} C_i}$$
Where $C_i$ denotes the credit assigned to course $i$, and $G_i$ represents the grade point earned in that course.

2.2. **Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA)**:
$$\text{CGPA} = \frac{\sum_{j=1}^{m} (C_j \times G_j)}{\sum_{j=1}^{m} C_j}$$
Computed across all successfully completed credits up to the latest semester.

---

## Section 3: Degree Classification & Honors
3.1. Degree awards are categorized upon completion of all 160 prescribed credits:
- **First Class with Distinction**: $\text{CGPA} \ge 8.50$, completed within four consecutive years, with zero backlog history.
- **First Class**: $\text{CGPA} \ge 6.75$ achieved within five consecutive academic years.
- **Second Class**: $\text{CGPA} < 6.75$ with all credits earned.
3.2. **Maximum Permissible Duration**: Students must finish the 4-year B.Tech program within a maximum of **six (6) academic years** ($N + 2$).

---

## Section 4: Fast-Track & Remedial Academic Policies
4.1. Fast-track semester overload allows students with $\text{CGPA} \ge 8.50$ to register for up to six (6) additional credits per semester starting from Semester 5.
4.2. Students with active backlogs are restricted to a maximum of 24 credits per semester, including arrears.
