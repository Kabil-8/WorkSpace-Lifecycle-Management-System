# EduSphere Campus Placement & Career Internship Policy 2026

**Document ID**: `univ-pol-place-2026`  
**Category**: `placement_policy`  
**Applicability**: Final Year & Pre-Final Year Graduating Batches  
**Effective Cycle**: Campus Recruitment Season 2025–2026  
**Issuing Authority**: Career Development Centre & Corporate Relations Cell (CDC)  

---

## Section 1: Standard Placement Eligibility Criteria
1.1. Registration for on-campus placement drives is subject to the following universal criteria:
- **Minimum Cumulative CGPA**: **6.50** without rounding off.
- **Active Backlog Limit**: **Zero (0) active backlogs** at the time of company registration. History of cleared backlogs is acceptable unless explicitly restricted by Tier-1 corporate partners.
- **Attendance Requirement**: Minimum **75% aggregate attendance** in pre-placement training modules, soft skills workshops, and domain technical seminars.
- **ATS Resume Clearance**: An approved ATS Resume Score of **at least 75%** verified via the EduSphere Career Intelligence Engine.

---

## Section 2: Company Classification & Salary Tiers
2.1. Visiting corporate organizations are categorized into three competitive recruitment tiers:
- **Tier 1 (Dream & Super-Dream Corporates)**: CTC **≥ ₹10.0 LPA** (e.g., Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Atlassian, Zoho Product Division).
- **Tier 2 (Core Engineering & Growth Tech)**: CTC between **₹6.0 LPA and ₹9.9 LPA** (e.g., TCS Digital, Infosys Specialty, Cognizant Elevate, L&T Technology).
- **Tier 3 (Mass Recruiters & IT Services)**: CTC between **₹3.6 LPA and ₹5.9 LPA** (e.g., TCS Ninja, Wipro Turbo, Accenture, Capgemini).

---

## Section 3: One-Student One-Offer & Dream Offer Rules
3.1. **Core Rule**: Once a student secures a confirmed placement offer in Tier 3 or Tier 2, they are automatically de-registered from subsequent recruitment drives in the same or lower tier.
3.2. **Dream Offer Upgrade**: A student holding an offer in Tier 3 or Tier 2 is entitled to contest for **one (1) Dream / Super-Dream Offer** in Tier 1 (CTC $\ge$ ₹10.0 LPA).
3.3. Upon receiving a Tier 1 Dream Offer, the candidate must confirm acceptance within 48 hours; their previous offer is formally surrendered to the next student on the waitlist.

---

## Section 4: Code of Conduct & Offer Reneging Penalties
4.1. Strict professionalism is mandated during on-campus interviews:
- Failure to attend an interview after registering without 24-hour advance written intimation results in a **two-week suspension** from all ongoing campus recruitment drives.
- Malpractice, identity misrepresentation, or falsified resume achievements during coding rounds triggers immediate permanent blacklisting from campus placement assistance.
