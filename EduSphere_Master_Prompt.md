### How EDEN Integrates Into Every Module (implement all of these as real, functioning behaviors)

**Academic**
- Dashboard: greets by name, shows a personalized daily roadmap, mood-based motivational messages, pending-work highlights, AI productivity insights, achievement-unlock animations.

- Smart Notes: summarizes, generates flashcards and quizzes, explains diagrams, converts notes to mind maps, predicts likely exam questions.

- Coding Platform: live bug detection, optimization suggestions, algorithm explanations, time-complexity prediction, alternate-approach suggestions, submission celebrations.
- MCQ Engine: post-quiz breakdown of wrong answers, weak concepts, and recommended revision.
- Study Planner: auto-generates daily/weekly/monthly/exam/placement/hackathon plans.
- Flashcards: spaced-repetition scheduling decided by EDEN.
- AI Tutor: shifts persona (Teacher / Friend / Mentor / Exam Coach / Career Guide) based on context.

**Career**
- Resume Builder: writes and improves resumes, raises ATS score, suggests projects and certifications.
- ATS Analyzer: quantifies improvement suggestions (e.g., "Adding Docker and AWS could raise your ATS score by ~15%").
- Portfolio Builder: auto-syncs projects, skills, achievements, certificates, GitHub, LinkedIn.
- Mock Interviews: transforms into role-specific interviewer personas (e.g., Google Interviewer, Amazon Interviewer, HR Manager, Network Architect).
- Internship Portal: ranks opportunities against profile, skills, goals, location, interests.
- Placement Portal: predicts placement probability pre-application.
- Career Universe: 3D/interactive navigator through skills and career paths.
- Life GPS: daily-updating career trajectory predictions.

**Collaboration**
- Team Workspace: suggests best teammates by skill, availability, and past collaboration.
- Kanban: predicts late tasks and auto-reprioritizes.
- Chat: real-time translation, AI summaries, extracted action items, smart replies.
- Whiteboard: converts freehand sketches into clean digital diagrams.
- GitHub Integration: explains commits, reviews PRs, tracks contribution quality.
- Meeting Scheduler: finds optimal times, generates agendas and minutes automatically.
- Discussion Forum: highlights best answers, detects duplicates, summarizes threads.
- File Sharing: auto-categorizes, indexes, and summarizes documents.
